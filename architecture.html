Architecture

Built on Cloudflare,
on the free plan.

The game itself is plain HTML and JavaScript, served by Vercel. Everything that has to be shared between players runs on Cloudflare: one Worker for the API, one Durable Object per race room, and a D1 database for leaderboards and shared tracks.

Core

What runs, and why

Workers In use

What
The single entry point: health check, leaderboard and track APIs, and the WebSocket route into a room.
Why
Runs close to every player, checks the origin and validates every request before it touches the database.
Free tier
100,000 requests a day, 10 ms CPU per request. A room join is one request.

Durable Objects In use

What
One RaceRoom per room code. It holds that room's sockets and relays poses, countdowns, finishes and chat.
Why
Everyone with the same code reaches the same object, so it can enforce the 8 driver cap, stop one player posing as another, and rate limit floods.
Free tier
100,000 requests a day; incoming WebSocket messages count 20 to 1, outgoing are free.

WebSockets In use

What
The live link between each player and their room. Poses go 6 to 20 times a second depending on room size.
Why
Low latency both ways without polling. HTTP is only used for things that happen once, like posting a time.
Limits
600 KB per message, 60 messages a second per sender with bursts of 120.

D1 In use

What
Race results, the daily track board, the daily typing board, and shared drawn tracks behind short links.
Why
Small relational data with rankings that have to be computed on the server, never on the client.
Free tier
5 million rows read and 100,000 written a day. A finished race writes one row.

R2 Not used

What
Object storage for large files: replays, uploaded assets.
Why not
A shared track is about 2 KB, which fits in D1. R2 also needs an R2 subscription added in the dashboard first.
Free tier
10 GB-month storage, 1 million Class A and 10 million Class B operations a month, free egress.
Optional

Left out on purpose

Turnstile Not used

What
Bot check for public actions.
Why not
Needs a site key created in the dashboard. Results are already checked by the server and rate limited per IP.
Free tier
Unlimited challenges, up to 20 widgets.

Queues Not used

What
Background jobs.
Why not
Nothing in the game needs to happen later. Gameplay never goes through a queue.
Free tier
10,000 operations a day.

KV Not used

What
Small cached values.
Why not
The daily track is generated from the date in the browser, so there is nothing to cache.
Free tier
100,000 reads and 1,000 writes a day.

Workers AI Not used

What
A possible post-race coach.
Why not
Kept out of the game loop. A future feature, if it adds real value.
Free tier
10,000 Neurons a day.
